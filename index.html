Academic Paper Integrity Tool and Retraction Audit Platform

RetractScan scans academic manuscripts, journal archives, and systematic reviews to detect retracted citations in real time using Crossref, PubMed, and OpenAlex. Ensure clinical evidence safety and protect publication integrity before acceptance.

Four-Layer Retraction Detection Methodology

RetractScan applies a multi-channel empirical verification architecture to ensure 100% retraction notice capture without false alarms:

  • Layer 1: Crossref REST API Retraction Index — Continuous synchronization with publisher Crossmark updates, identifying official retraction notices, expressions of concern, and corrigenda.
  • Layer 2: PubMed E-Utilities & MeSH Matching — Direct verification against the National Library of Medicine (NLM) PubMed retraction index, parsing PubMed Central full-text notices and MeSH status tags.
  • Layer 3: OpenAlex Multidisciplinary Knowledge Graph — Cross-domain verification across 250M+ scientific publications, mapping secondary citation propagation across global academic disciplines.
  • Layer 4: COPE Corrigenda & Meta-Analysis Contamination Analysis — Temporal indexing distinguishing pre-retraction from post-retraction citations, with automated COPE erratum notice drafting.
